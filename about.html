About Kiwi Nails Fasteners.

Kiwi Nails Fasteners Ltd manufactures and distributes its fastening products in the New Zealand market — as both a wholesaler and a manufacturer.

Built in partnership with NZ builders.

We are committed to working in partnership with our customers to grow our business together — by providing quality products and reliable service.

From our East Tamaki, Auckland warehouse we supply builders, framers, fencers, roofers, and the hardware merchants who serve them across the North and South Islands.

Manufacturing

Manufactured in our own China factory. Warehoused in Auckland.

Kiwi Nails owns and operates its own fastener manufacturing line in China — not a third-party contract — so we retain complete quality control and only use raw material with proper Mill Certification.

Finished goods are then shipped to our East Tamaki, Auckland warehouse, our local New Zealand storage and distribution hub. Trade customers and merchants are supplied from this Auckland warehouse — same-day picking on most orders.

  • Own manufacturing line in China — not contracted
  • Raw material with Mill Certification only
  • Mass-scale capacity + in-house R&D
  • Custom sizes, packs and coatings on request
  • Local Auckland warehouse for prompt NZ supply
