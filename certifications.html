BRANZ Appraised. ISO 9001 manufactured.

Our structural hardware is BRANZ Appraised against NZS 3604, our 90 × 3.15 mm bright framing nail holds a BRANZ Standards Test report, and our entire manufacturing line operates under an ISO 9001 certified quality management system.

BRANZ Appraised — issued 25 Nov 2025

Two current BRANZ Appraisals

Our Joist Hangers and CPCs hold full BRANZ Appraisal Certificates — the highest tier of product certification in New Zealand for structural building products.

BRANZ
Appraisal No. 1132 · 25 Nov 2025

Kiwi Nails Joist Hangers

Alternative connection of curtail joist to trimmer, or trimmer to trimming joist, as described in NZS 3604, for timber-framed structures. Galvanised, four sizes.

BRANZ
Appraisal No. 1133 · 25 Nov 2025

Kiwi Nails CPCs (Concealed Plate Cleats)

Alternative connection in roof construction to tie a pair of rafters to the ridge board, and rafter-to-top-plate connection, as described in NZS 3604. Galvanised, two sizes.
