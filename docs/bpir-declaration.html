3. Product range covered

This declaration covers all SKUs supplied by Kiwi Nails Fasteners Ltd across:

  • Hardware — Brace strap, CPC, joist hangers, stud strap, washer, threaded rod, soakers, W-Flash, pin anchors, drive pin, wire dog
  • Screws — Decking, bugle batten, HWF (Tek), chipboard, purlin, jolt-head screws
  • Collated Gun Nails — 34° paper-collated D-head, jolt-head weatherboard, round-head, joist-hanger nails, staples, brads
  • Coil Nails — Round-head, jolt-head weatherboard, shingle roofing, industrial, roofing tile
  • Bolts — Engineering, coach, coach screw, screw bolt, through bolt
  • Loose Nails — Flat-head, jolt-head, rose-head, clinch, decking, fibre cement, fencing, clout, underlay, product, soaker, panel pin, roofing, fencing batten staple

4. Intended use

Products are intended for use in light timber-framed construction (NZS 3604), structural steel works (AS/NZS 1554), roofing and cladding (NZS 3604, E2/AS1), fencing, boat-building and general carpentry. Material grade and fastener selection must follow the project's structural engineer's design and the relevant NZ Building Code clauses.
