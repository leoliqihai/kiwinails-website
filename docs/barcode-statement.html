GS1-NZ Barcode Range Statement

Confirmation of GS1 NZ membership and allocated GTIN range. Document reference KNF-GS1-2026-05.

Kiwi Nails Fasteners Ltd is a registered member of GS1 New Zealand. All retail and trade pack barcodes are issued from our allocated GTIN range under the company prefix 9420052.

This guarantees that every Kiwi Nails SKU has a unique, NZ-registered EAN-13 barcode that is scan-compatible with all major NZ POS systems — including Vend, Lightspeed, Pronto, Triquestra, Citrix RetailIQ and Counterpoint.

Format

Pattern9 420052 XXXXXX C
Country prefix942 — New Zealand
Company prefix0052 — Kiwi Nails Fasteners Ltd allocation
Item referenceXXXXXX — Unique 6-digit Kiwi Nails SKU index
Check digitC — Standard EAN-13 check digit (modulo 10)

Coverage

Every Kiwi Nails Fasteners Ltd product pack carries a registered EAN-13 barcode. As of this issue we operate 412+ active SKUs across six product families (Hardware, Screws, Collated Gun Nails, Coil Nails, Bolts, Loose Nails).

Use restrictions: Retailers must scan and price each unit individually. Re-packing under a different barcode (PLU) is permitted but the original Kiwi Nails barcode must remain visible on the outer carton for traceability.

Verification

The complete GTIN list for current Kiwi Nails SKUs can be requested by emailing info@kiwinails.co.nz. GS1 New Zealand maintains an authoritative record of the 9420052 prefix allocation; verification can be performed at gs1nz.org.
