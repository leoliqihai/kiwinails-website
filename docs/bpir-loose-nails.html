6. Warranty

One-year replacement warranty on manufacturing defects. Warranty does not cover damage caused by incorrect installation, use outside the appraisal scope, or exposure conditions beyond the durability zone of the material grade selected.

Installation responsibility: Selection of fastener size, grade and quantity must be made by the project designer / structural engineer in accordance with NZS 3604 or the project's specific structural design. Always refer to the BRANZ Appraisal certificate and technical literature in full.
