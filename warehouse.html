Real photos from our Auckland warehouse.

The product images you see on the rest of the site are studio shots of our actual products with AI-enhanced lighting and shadow — full-quality professional product photography is currently underway. In the meantime, these photos taken inside our East Tamaki warehouse show the real range, stock depth and operation.

6B Lorien Place · East Tamaki · Auckland

Our New Zealand warehouse

Roughly 1,200 m² of pallet-racked stock across all six product families. Picking, packing and dispatch all run from this site — orders typically leave within 24 hours during working days.

Main aisle · stock depth

Three high-bay rows of pallet racking. Joist hangers, brace straps, coil nails, collated framing nails and bolt cartons all kept in inventory for same-day picking.

Loose-nail & hardware racking

Boxed loose nails, fence staples and structural hardware stacked by SKU. Lower shelves are pick-faces for partial-carton orders; upper shelves hold pallet reserves.

Floor & office

Our office sits at the front of the warehouse — sales, despatch and accounts all under one roof. The forklift area is right behind the office for fast pallet loading onto courier trucks.

Forklift & loading

Onsite forklift operator for pallet movements. Most outgoing orders ship via NZ Couriers / Mainfreight on weekday afternoons.

About the product images on this site: Every product image you see across the site (on category pages, sub-range pages and the per-SKU pages) is a photograph of our actual physical product — the source shot was taken in our studio, then re-lit and re-touched with AI for clean studio presentation. Full-quality professional product photography is currently underway and will replace the AI-enhanced versions as it becomes available.
